## Using the app

Choose Reader, Journalist, or Editor when registering.
Application roles do not grant access to Django admin.

### Editors

1. Register as an Editor and log in.
2. Open My publishers and select Create publisher.
3. Enter a name and save. You are automatically added as an editor.
4. Open Manage team to assign registered Editors and Journalists.
5. Keep yourself selected when saving the team.
6. Use Review queue to review and approve pending articles.

Editors can manage articles belonging to their publishers and can
also review independent articles.

### Journalists

1. Register as a Journalist.
2. Ask an editor of your publisher to add you through Manage team.
3. Open My articles to create and manage your articles.
4. Select your publisher, or leave it empty for an independent article.

New and edited articles require editorial approval before appearing
on Home or in readers' feeds.

### Readers

1. Register as a Reader.
2. Open Subscriptions to follow publishers and journalists.
3. Open My feed to view their approved articles.

### Newsletters and notifications

Journalists and Editors can create newsletters containing approved
articles.

Article approval notifies subscribers
